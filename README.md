# ElectroSmash Archive

A static, hostable-anywhere mirror of the most popular articles from
[Electrosmash.com](https://electrosmash.com), rebuilt from a 2022
[Wayback Machine capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20220504054643/https://www.electrosmash.com/)
after the original site went offline.

Open `index.html` to browse. Each article was rescued from archive.org, cleaned up,
and rebuilt as its own self-contained page (`<slug>.html`) with the original images
restored at full resolution (`images/`), MathJax-rendered formulas where the source
used them, and internal cross-links pointed at the local pages instead of dead/archived
URLs. Links to pages outside this archive (forum, shop, unrelated articles) were removed.

It is *NOT a complete backup* of the site — just these 29 pedal/chip/amp analyses.
For a more comprehensive (but much rougher) archive, visit
[archive.org](https://web.archive.org/web/20220504054643/https://www.electrosmash.com/).

This site used to be just a folder of PDF exports (one per article, made from saved
Chrome pages) plus a plain `index.html`. Those are kept for reference in `pdf/`.
