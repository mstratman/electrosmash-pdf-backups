ElectroSmash Archive

ElectroSmash.com published some of the best circuit-analysis articles on the web for guitar pedals, amps, and the chips inside them — but the original site has been unreachable for a while. This is an unofficial mirror of its most popular articles, rebuilt from a 2022 Wayback Machine capture so they're readable again, with the original images restored at full size.

It is not a complete backup of the site — just these 29 articles. For a more comprehensive (but much rougher) archive, browse electrosmash.com on archive.org directly.
